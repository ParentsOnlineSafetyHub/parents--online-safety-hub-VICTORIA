Strong kids are not kids who never struggle.

Recovery Skill
RECOVER. LEARN. GROW.

What resilience actually means

  • Making mistakes and learning
  • Getting hurt and recovering
  • Facing challenges and continuing
  • Asking for help when needed
  • Trying again after setbacks

What resilience is NOT

  • Pretending everything is okay
  • Never feeling upset
  • Never needing help
  • Being tough all the time
