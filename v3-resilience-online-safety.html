Resilience & Online Safety

Resilience is the ability to recover without giving up.

Resilience Safety Skill
SETBACKS ARE NOT STOP SIGNS
Children who recover from disappointment, embarrassment and mistakes are less likely to be controlled by fear.

Without resilience

  • Children avoid challenges
  • Mistakes become disasters
  • Embarrassment becomes panic
  • Failure becomes identity
  • Pressure becomes overwhelming

With resilience

  • Mistakes become lessons
  • Problems become solvable
  • Embarrassment passes
  • Challenges become growth
  • Pressure becomes manageable

The resilience pathway

Problem
↓
Pause
↓
Learn
↓
Adjust
↓
Grow stronger

Final POSH reminder

Resilient children recover faster. Children who recover faster are harder to manipulate.
