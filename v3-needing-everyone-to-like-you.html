POSH

Needing Everyone To Like You

One of the biggest online risks is the belief that everyone must approve of you.

Children who fear rejection often ignore red flags to avoid upsetting people.

What this can look like

  • Saying yes when they want to say no
  • Replying when uncomfortable
  • Ignoring warning signs
  • Keeping unsafe secrets
  • Accepting poor treatment

The reality

Not everyone will like you.

Not everyone should like you.

You do not need everyone's approval.

You only need healthy relationships.

The POSH Rule

Being respected is more important than being liked.
Being safe is more important than being popular.

Final POSH Reminder

Children who can tolerate disapproval become much harder to pressure, manipulate, or groom.
