POSH

Circle of Control

Most anxiety comes from trying to control things you cannot.

Children become stronger when they focus on what belongs inside their control.

Outside Your Control

  • What others say
  • What others think
  • Whether people like you
  • Rumours
  • Other people's choices
  • Other people's behaviour

Inside Your Control

  • Your response
  • Your words
  • Your actions
  • Who you talk to
  • Who you trust
  • Whether you ask for help
Power lives inside your circle.

Final POSH Reminder

You cannot control everything.

You can always control something.

Focus there.
