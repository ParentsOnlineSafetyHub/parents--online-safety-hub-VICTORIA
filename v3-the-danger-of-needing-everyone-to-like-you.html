The Danger of Needing Everyone To Like You

One of the most freeing lessons a child can learn:
Not everyone has to like you.

Confidence Skill
RESPECT IS BETTER THAN APPROVAL
Children who need everybody's approval often give away boundaries to keep it.

What happens when approval becomes the goal

  • Saying yes when you want to say no
  • Hiding your opinions
  • Tolerating poor treatment
  • Ignoring warning signs
  • Following unhealthy groups
  • Fear of disappointing others

The healthier belief

Not everyone will agree with me

Not everyone will understand me

Not everyone will like me

That is okay

Freedom pathway

Know your values
↓
Respect yourself
↓
Set boundaries
↓
Accept disagreement
↓
Stay safe
The goal is not to be liked by everyone. The goal is to be safe, respectful and true to yourself.
