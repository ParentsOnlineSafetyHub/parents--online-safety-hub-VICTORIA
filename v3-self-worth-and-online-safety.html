Self Worth & Online Safety

Children who know their value are harder to exploit.

Self Worth Safety Skill
MY VALUE DOESN'T COME FROM STRANGERS
Predators, bullies and manipulators often target children who are searching for validation.

What low self-worth sounds like

  • "Nobody likes me."
  • "I need people to approve of me."
  • "If they leave I'll have nobody."
  • "I should do what they want."
  • "I don't deserve better."

What healthy self-worth sounds like

  • "I deserve respect."
  • "I can say no."
  • "I don't need everyone's approval."
  • "I can leave unsafe situations."
  • "I am valuable even when others disagree."

Why predators target low self-worth

Validation feels powerful

Attention feels addictive

Approval becomes leverage

Fear of rejection increases compliance

Children who know their worth are much harder to control.
