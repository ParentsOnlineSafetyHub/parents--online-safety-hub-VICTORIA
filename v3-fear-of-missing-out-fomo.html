Fear Of Missing Out & Online Safety

FOMO can make children ignore their own safety.
The fear of being left out can feel stronger than the warning signs in front of them.

Children often stay in unsafe chats, games, groups, or online friendships because they are scared of being excluded.
Confidence & pressure cluster
LEFT OUT FEELS LIKE DANGER
When belonging feels threatened, children may rush decisions, ignore discomfort, or say yes just to stay included.

What FOMO looks like online

  • Staying in group chats that make them uncomfortable
  • Joining games or apps because everyone else is there
  • Replying late at night so they do not miss anything
  • Feeling anxious when they are offline
  • Ignoring bad behaviour to stay accepted
  • Hiding online activity because they fear losing access

The FOMO risk pathway

Fear of missing out
↓
Pressure to stay included
↓
Ignoring discomfort
↓
Poor boundaries
↓
Unsafe choices

What children need to learn

You can miss out and still be okay.

You can leave a chat and still have friends.

You can say no and still belong.

You can be offline and still matter.

Parent scripts

“You do not need to be available all the time to be a good friend.”
“Missing one chat does not mean you are losing people.”
“If a group only accepts you when you ignore your safety, it is not a safe group.”
“You are allowed to step back.”

Final POSH reminder

FOMO creates urgency.

Urgency weakens boundaries.

Strong children can pause, step back, and still know they matter.

Belonging should never cost safety.
