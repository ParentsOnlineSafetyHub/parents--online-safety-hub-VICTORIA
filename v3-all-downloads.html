How to use these downloads

POSH downloads are designed to reduce overwhelm. You do not need everything at once. Choose the resource that matches the moment your family, school, team or community is facing.

1. Start calm

Read the guide before a crisis if possible. Children are safer when adults already know the words to use.

2. Practise the script

Use the parent and safe-adult scripts to keep children talking instead of hiding, freezing or feeling blamed.

3. Take the next step

Move from awareness into action: device settings, family rules, safety checks, evidence steps or reporting pathways.

Devices, Apps, Games and Platforms
