How Your Assumptions Shape Your World

The story you tell yourself affects how you feel.
Many emotions begin with assumptions, not facts.

Critical Thinking Skill
FACTS FIRST. STORIES SECOND.
Children often react to what they think is happening rather than what is actually happening.

Assumption examples

"They didn't reply. They must hate me."

"They laughed. They must be laughing at me."

"I wasn't invited. Nobody likes me."

"They disagreed with me. I'm a bad person."

Ask better questions

  • What facts do I actually know?
  • What am I assuming?
  • Could there be another explanation?
  • Would I tell a friend this story?
  • Am I reacting to facts or feelings?

Healthier thinking pathway

Event happens
↓
Check facts
↓
Challenge assumptions
↓
Respond calmly
↓
Better outcome
The assumptions you repeat become the world you see.
