POSH

Assume Positive Intent

Most people are not thinking about you as much as you think.
One of the healthiest habits children can learn is assuming positive intent until proven otherwise.

Mindset Skill
DON'T INVENT ENEMIES
Many worries, arguments and emotional reactions begin with assumptions that may not be true.

What positive intent means

  • Giving people the benefit of the doubt
  • Not assuming bad motives immediately
  • Considering alternative explanations
  • Reducing unnecessary conflict
  • Protecting your own peace of mind

Two possible paths

Someone ignores your message
↓
"They hate me"
↓
Stress & anger

Someone ignores your message
↓
"Maybe they're busy"
↓
Calm response
Assuming positive intent protects your peace, even when you're wrong.
