POSH

Assume Positive Intent & Online Safety

Not every message is an attack.
Teaching children to assume positive intent can reduce conflict, overthinking, anger, and emotional reactions online.

Positive mindset safety skill
PAUSE BEFORE YOU ASSUME
When children assume the worst, their emotions react before their thinking does.
POSH rule:
Assume positive intent first. If something still feels unsafe, pause, check, and ask for help.

Why this matters

Negative assumptions create emotional pressure.

Emotional pressure creates fast reactions.

Fast reactions create online conflict.

Assuming the worst gives other people control over your mood.

What children often assume

  • “They hate me.”
  • “They ignored me on purpose.”
  • “They are laughing at me.”
  • “Everyone is against me.”
  • “I need to respond right now.”
The assumption may feel real, but that does not mean it is true.

The safer mindset

Maybe they were busy.

Maybe they worded it badly.

Maybe I misunderstood.

Maybe I should wait before replying.

Maybe I do not need to react at all.

The POSH pause pattern

Message received
↓
Feeling starts

What to teach your child

“I don’t have to decide what they meant straight away.”
“I can wait before I respond.”
“Not everything is personal.”
“If it still feels unsafe, I can ask a safe adult.”
