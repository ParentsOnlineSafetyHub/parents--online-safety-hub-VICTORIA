POSH

Owning Your Next Step

You don't need all the answers.
You only need the next safe step.

Overwhelm shrinks when children stop focusing on everything and start focusing on the next action.
Recovery Skill
ONE STEP AT A TIME
Safety improves when children stop trying to solve everything at once.

Why children freeze

"I have to fix everything."

"Everything is ruined."

"I don't know what to do."

Big problems feel smaller when broken into steps.

The POSH Recovery Formula

Problem happens
↓
Pause
↓
Find next safe step
↓
Take action
↓
Repeat

Examples

  • Being pressured → stop replying
  • Sent something unsafe → tell trusted adult
  • Being bullied → save evidence
  • Feeling overwhelmed → ask for help

Child script

"I don't need to solve everything today."
"I only need my next safe step."

Parent script

"We don't need every answer right now."
"Let's find the next safe step together."

Final POSH Reminder

Control is not controlling everything.

Control starts with the next decision.

One safe step is enough.
