POSH

Don't Give Negative People Free Rent In Your Head

Every minute spent thinking about negative people is energy they didn't earn.

Resilience Skill
YOUR ATTENTION IS VALUABLE
Not everyone deserves your time, focus or emotional energy.

What happens when we obsess

  • Stress increases
  • Confidence drops
  • Anger grows
  • Focus disappears
  • Mood worsens

The healthier approach

  • Notice negativity
  • Learn from it if needed
  • Set boundaries
  • Move forward
  • Protect your peace
The less attention you give negativity, the less power it has.
