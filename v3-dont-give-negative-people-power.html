POSH

Don’t Give Negative People Power

Some people want a reaction.
The safest response is often not giving them one.

Mindset protection page
YOUR REACTION IS YOUR POWER
When someone online can control your mood, they can influence your choices.
POSH rule:
Do not hand your peace to people who are trying to upset you.

Why negative people push buttons

They may want attention.

They may want control.

They may want drama.

They may want screenshots of your reaction.

A reaction can become their reward.

What giving power looks like

  • Replying while angry
  • Trying to prove yourself
  • Arguing for hours
  • Sending screenshots to everyone
  • Letting one comment ruin the day
  • Changing your behaviour because someone was cruel

What keeping power looks like

Pause before replying.

Block without explaining.

Screenshot if needed.

Tell a safe adult if it escalates.

Do not chase people who enjoy upsetting you.

The control chain

They provoke
↓
You react
↓
They get attention
↓
They push again
↓
You lose peace

The power-back chain

They provoke
↓
You pause
↓
You do not feed it
↓
You block or report
↓
You stay in control

Scripts for kids
