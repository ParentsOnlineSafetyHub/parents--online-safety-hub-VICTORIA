What POSH teaches

One safety system, taught in plain language

The digital world changes quickly, but many safety patterns repeat. Children need words, adults need patterns, and families need calm systems that work before fear takes over.

Children learn safety skills

Pause before clicking, notice pressure, ask for help, set boundaries, speak up, recover from mistakes and trust their own warning signs.

Adults learn the pattern

Private contact, secrecy, emotional pressure, gifts, shame, isolation, hidden accounts and off-platform movement can appear across apps, games and real life.

Communities learn together

Parents, teachers, grandparents, carers, coaches and safe adults become more useful when they use the same calm safety language.

How the books strengthen each other

The series follows the way risk actually builds

POSH is not just a list of apps. It teaches the skills beneath safety: thinking, regulation, confidence, boundaries, communication and trusted-adult response.

↓

The platform may change. The pattern often stays the same. POSH teaches people to recognise the pattern, not just memorise the app name.

Built for everyone around children

POSH is for every generation and every learning style. You do not need to be a technology expert to become useful. You need simple patterns, calm language and a willingness to practise.

Families

Parents, step-parents, grandparents, aunties, uncles, carers, older siblings and family friends.

Schools and teams

Teachers, support staff, coaches, youth workers, mentors, activity leaders and club communities.

Children and young people

Age-appropriate safety language that helps children recognise pressure and ask for help earlier.

Some situations need action now

The book series teaches long-term safety. Urgent situations need calm action immediately.

Move faster if there are threats, blackmail, sexual requests, image pressure, requests for secrecy or talk about meeting in person.

Do not shame. Do not panic. Do not delete evidence first. Slow the contact down and get help.
