The Online Validation Trap

Validation feels good.
The danger begins when children need it to feel okay.

Predators, scammers, bullies and manipulators often begin with validation.

Why validation feels powerful

  • Attention feels rewarding
  • Praise feels exciting
  • Recognition feels important
  • Approval feels safe

The Validation Trap

Attention
↓
Praise
↓
Dependence
↓
People pleasing
↓
Manipulation

Healthy validation

  • I enjoy compliments
  • I don't need compliments
  • I value myself without approval
  • I know my worth

Final POSH Reminder

Children who know their value are harder to manipulate with attention.
