Comparison Is The Thief Of Confidence

The internet shows everyone's highlight reel.
Children often compare their real life to someone else's best moments.

Comparison rarely creates confidence. It usually creates insecurity.

What comparison sounds like

  • They're prettier than me
  • They're more popular than me
  • They have more friends
  • They get more likes
  • Their life looks better

The truth about social media

People post victories.

People hide struggles.

People filter reality.

People edit their lives.

What confidence sounds like

  • I don't need to be them.
  • I can improve myself.
  • I have my own strengths.
  • I am growing.
  • I am enough.

Final POSH Reminder

The only person your child should compare themselves to is who they were yesterday.
