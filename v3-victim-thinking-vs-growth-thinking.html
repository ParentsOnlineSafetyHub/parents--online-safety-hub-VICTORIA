Victim Thinking vs Growth Thinking

Your mindset affects your safety.
The way children explain problems often determines how they respond to them.

POSH Truth:
Children who believe they have choices are harder to manipulate than children who believe they have none.
Mindset & Resilience
FROM "WHY ME?" TO "WHAT NOW?"
Growth thinking doesn't ignore problems. It helps children respond to them safely.

What is victim thinking?

Believing nothing can improve

Believing you have no choices

Believing everything happens to you

Believing other people control everything

Victim thinking removes power.

What victim thinking sounds like

  • "There's no point."
  • "Nothing ever works."
  • "Everyone is against me."
  • "I can't do anything."
  • "Why bother trying?"
The more powerless a child feels, the easier they become to manipulate.

What growth thinking sounds like

  • "What's my next step?"
  • "What can I learn?"
  • "Who can help me?"
  • "What can I control?"
  • "How do I improve this?"
Growth thinking creates options.

Why predators prefer victim thinking

Child feels alone
↓
Child feels powerless
↓
Hope decreases
↓
Dependence increases
↓
Manipulation becomes easier
