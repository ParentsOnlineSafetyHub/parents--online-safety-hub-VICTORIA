POSH

What You Focus On Grows

Attention is like water.
Whatever children repeatedly focus on tends to grow.

Mindset Skill
ATTENTION CREATES DIRECTION
Children cannot always choose what happens. They can choose what they focus on next.

Negative focus

  • Fear
  • Drama
  • Embarrassment
  • Rejection
  • Conflict
  • Worst-case thinking
What receives attention often becomes larger.

Growth focus

  • Solutions
  • Learning
  • Recovery
  • Support
  • Progress
  • Resilience
Focus influences emotional recovery.

Online example

Child gets bullied.

Focus: "Everyone hates me."

Result: Anxiety grows.

Focus: "Who can help me?"

Result: Solutions grow.

Parent lesson

You don't need to deny reality.
You simply decide where attention goes next.

Final POSH Reminder

What you feed grows.

What you practise strengthens.

What you focus on expands.

Feed solutions, not panic.
