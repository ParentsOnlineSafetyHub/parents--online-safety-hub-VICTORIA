POSH

Not Everything Is About You

One of the biggest causes of anxiety is believing everything relates to you.
Most of the time, people are busy thinking about themselves, not judging you.

POSH Resilience Skill:
Teach children how to separate facts from assumptions and stop carrying problems that may not belong to them.
Mental resilience skill
MOST PEOPLE ARE THINKING ABOUT THEMSELVES
Children often believe every comment, look, message, silence, or behaviour is somehow connected to them.
POSH Truth:
Most people are worrying about their own lives, not yours.

Why this matters

Personalising creates anxiety.

Personalising creates insecurity.

Personalising creates conflict.

Personalising creates unnecessary stress.

Children often suffer from problems they accidentally create in their own minds.

Examples children experience

  • A friend doesn't reply immediately
  • Someone laughs nearby
  • A group doesn't invite them
  • A teacher seems frustrated
  • A friend acts differently one day
  • A social media post feels personal
Many children immediately assume these things are about them.

The anxiety cycle

Something happens
↓
"It must be about me"
↓
Worry grows
↓
Confidence drops
↓
Stress increases

The healthier approach

Something happens
↓
Maybe it isn't about me
↓
Stay calm
↓
Wait for facts
↓
Less stress

Questions children can ask themselves

Do I know this is about me?
What evidence do I have?
Could there be another explanation?
Am I guessing or do I know?
Would I think this about someone else?

What is usually happening?

  • The friend is busy
  • The teacher had a hard day
  • The person is distracted
  • The message wasn't aimed at them
  • Someone forgot
  • Life simply got in the way
The simplest explanation is often the correct one.

What parents can teach

"Let's look at the facts."
"What evidence do we have?"
"Could there be another explanation?"
"Are we assuming or do we know?"
"Let's wait before jumping to conclusions."

A powerful life lesson

You are important.

You matter.

But you are not the centre of everyone else's thoughts.

Most people are focused on themselves.

Understanding this creates freedom.
