POSH

Assume Positive Intent

Most stress starts with a story we create in our own mind.
Assuming positive intent helps children reduce anxiety, avoid conflict, and stay emotionally stronger.

POSH Resilience Skill:
When we don't know someone's intentions, choosing the most positive explanation is often healthier than choosing the most negative one.
Mental resilience skill
DON'T CREATE ENEMIES IN YOUR HEAD
Many children automatically assume people are judging them, excluding them, talking about them, or trying to upset them.
POSH Truth:
Most people are not trying to hurt you.
Most people are simply busy living their own lives.

What assuming positive intent means

Looking for reasonable explanations first

Not assuming the worst immediately

Giving people the benefit of the doubt

Waiting for facts before creating a story

Assumptions create stress. Facts create clarity.

Examples children experience

  • A friend takes hours to reply
  • A teacher seems frustrated
  • Someone forgets to invite them
  • A message sounds short or blunt
  • A friend walks past without saying hello
  • A group conversation changes topic
Most children immediately assume these things are negative.

The negative assumption cycle

Something happens
↓
Assume the worst
↓
Stress grows
↓
Emotions take over
↓
Conflict or anxiety

The positive intent cycle

Something happens
↓
Look for other explanations
↓
Stay calm
↓
Wait for facts
↓
Better decisions

Why this matters online

  • Text messages have no facial expressions
  • Social media removes context
  • Short messages can sound rude when they aren't
  • Silence can feel personal when it isn't
  • Children often fill gaps with negative assumptions
The less information we have, the easier it is to invent a story.

Questions children can ask themselves

Do I know this was intentional?
Could there be another explanation?
What evidence do I actually have?
Am I guessing or do I know?
Would I want someone to assume the worst about me?

The hidden superpower

Less anxiety

Less overthinking

Fewer arguments

Better friendships

More emotional control

Assuming positive intent protects your peace more than it protects theirs.

What parents can teach

"What else could be true?"
"Let's not jump to conclusions."
"Maybe they didn't mean it that way."
"Let's wait for more information."
"Let's focus on facts, not guesses."

Important POSH reminder

Assuming positive intent does not mean ignoring bullying, manipulation, abuse, threats, or unsafe behaviour.

It simply means avoiding negative assumptions when the facts are unclear.

Kindness and caution can exist together.
