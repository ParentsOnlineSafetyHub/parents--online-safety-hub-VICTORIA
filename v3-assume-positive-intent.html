POSH

Assume Positive Intent

Most emotional stress starts with a story we tell ourselves.
When we don't know someone's intent, assuming the worst often creates unnecessary anxiety, anger, and conflict.

POSH Resilience Skill:
Teach children to slow down assumptions and give themselves the benefit of a healthier explanation.
Mental strength skill
UNKNOWN DOES NOT MEAN BAD
When we don't know why something happened, our brain often fills in the blanks. Unfortunately, it usually fills them with negative assumptions.
POSH Rule:
If you don't know someone's intent, don't automatically assume the worst.

Why assumptions matter

Assumptions create emotions.

Emotions influence decisions.

Decisions shape outcomes.

Bad assumptions often create unnecessary problems.

Most conflict starts with a misunderstanding, not bad intent.

What usually happens

No reply
↓
"They hate me"
↓
Anxiety or anger
↓
Conflict or stress
The emotion came from the assumption, not the event itself.

A healthier alternative

No reply
↓
"Maybe they're busy"
↓
No emotional reaction
↓
Move on with your day
The same event can create very different outcomes depending on the story we tell ourselves.

Examples children face online

  • A friend leaves you on read
  • Someone doesn't invite you to a game
  • A message sounds short
  • A friend seems distracted
  • A comment gets misunderstood
  • Someone doesn't respond immediately
There may be many explanations. The negative one is not automatically correct.

Why negative assumptions hurt

  • Create unnecessary anxiety
  • Increase anger and frustration
  • Damage friendships
  • Cause arguments
  • Lower self-esteem
  • Make children feel rejected when they are not
Assuming the worst rarely protects us. It usually hurts us.

The POSH thinking rule

Do I know their intent?

If not, I am guessing.

If I am guessing, I can choose a healthier explanation.

I don't need to create a problem that might not exist.

What to teach children

"Maybe there is another explanation."
"I don't know what they're thinking."
"I don't need to assume the worst."
"I can wait for more information."
"Not everything is about me."

Important reminder

Assuming positive intent does not mean ignoring red flags, bullying, manipulation, or unsafe behaviour.

Notice behaviour.

Pay attention to patterns.

Protect yourself when needed.

But don't create negative stories without evidence.

Healthy thinking means balanced thinking.

Final POSH reminder

You cannot read minds.

You cannot know everyone's intent.

You can choose the story you tell yourself.

Choose the one that gives you peace, not panic.

Unknown intent is not automatically bad intent.
