Which POSH Book Should I Read First?

Choose the best starting point in the POSH Digital Resilience Series based on what your family, school, team or community needs right now.

You do not need to read everything at once. Start with the concern in front of you. Build one safety skill. Practise one conversation. Then move to the next book when your family is ready.

Before choosing a book: is there urgent danger?

Books are for learning and prevention. Some situations need action now.

Use urgent help first if there are threats, blackmail, sexual requests, image pressure, requests to keep secrets, talk about meeting in person or fear that contact has already escalated.

Do not shame. Do not panic. Do not delete evidence first. Slow the contact down and get help.

Pick the book that matches the problem in front of you

The POSH series is built so families can start where the pressure is strongest. Each book connects back to the same safety system.

I want the full foundation

Start with Book 1. It explains the POSH safety mindset, digital resilience, calm communication, early action and the parent system.

Book 1: Raising Digitally Resilient Kids

I do not understand the games, apps and platforms

Start with Book 2. It explains the digital playground: games, chats, DMs, voice chat, livestreams, rewards, gifting and platform pathways.

Book 2: The Digital Playground

I am worried about grooming, secrecy or new risks

Start with Book 3. It focuses on new family risks, hidden pathways, manipulation, AI risks, secrecy and early red flags.

Book 3: The New Risks Families Face

My child has big feelings, shame or online stress

Start with Book 4. It teaches emotional regulation, calmer responses, shame reduction and how adults can keep children talking.

Book 4: Emotional Regulation Online

My child struggles with losing, waiting or hearing no

Start with Book 5. It teaches frustration tolerance, screen transitions, recovery, repair and resilience in a digital world.

Book 5: Frustration Tolerance

My child clicks, replies or reacts too quickly

Start with Book 6. It teaches pausing before links, downloads, messages, images, posts, comments, friend requests and pressure.

Book 6: Thinking Before Clicking

My child is easily influenced, tricked or pressured

Start with Book 7. It teaches manipulation, misinformation, fake content, rumours, scams, edited screenshots, AI content and social pressure.

Book 7: Manipulation & Misinformation

My child struggles with focus, planning or follow-through

Start with Book 8. It teaches executive functioning skills: planning, memory, focus, impulse control, transitions and organisation.

Book 8: Executive Functioning

My child needs more confidence to speak up

Start with Book 9. It teaches confidence as a safety skill: self-trust, courage, worth, asking for help and resisting pressure.

Book 9: Confidence Is a Safety Skill

Start with Book 10. It teaches privacy, respect, consent, saying no, personal information, relationships and trusted-adult boundaries.
