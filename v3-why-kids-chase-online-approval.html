Why Kids Chase Online Approval

Social media didn't create the need for approval.
It simply made approval visible, measurable and addictive.

Understanding Behaviour
LIKES FEEL LIKE ACCEPTANCE
For developing brains, digital approval can feel like proof of worth.

Why approval feels powerful

  • Humans naturally want belonging
  • Acceptance feels safe
  • Rejection feels painful
  • Recognition feels rewarding
  • Social status affects identity

Why online approval is different

  • It is immediate
  • It is public
  • It is measurable
  • It never stops
  • It encourages comparison

The hidden danger

Children may begin changing who they are

Children may hide their real opinions

Children may tolerate mistreatment

Children may seek attention from unsafe people

Approval should support identity — not create it.
